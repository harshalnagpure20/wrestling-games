## Placeholder character assets (temporary)

The GLB models and MP3s under `web/public/models` and `web/public/audio` are AI-generated assets
bundled with the chess fork. They are **placeholders only** — medieval soldiers standing in for
wrestlers during Phases 1–5. They will be replaced in Phase 6a by original MakeHuman bodies and
Quaternius / Mixamo animation clips (see `ASSET_SOURCES.md` in the parent folder).

Do not treat these as the shipped roster.
